Cubing Notes

CFOP solves the cube in four steps: the cross, the first two layers (F2L), orienting the last layer (OLL) and permuting it (PLL). Pick a step, start at Novice, and only move up a level once those algorithms feel automatic.

Every diagram is generated from its algorithm by a cube simulator, which also checks that each algorithm works. Hold the cube with yellow on top and green in front. Your "learned" marks are saved in this browser only.
